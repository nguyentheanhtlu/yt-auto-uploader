YT Auto Uploader

YT Auto Uploader is a private, internal desktop tool used by its owner to publish videos to YouTube channels that the owner manages.

The tool renders videos locally and uses the official YouTube Data API v3 to upload them, set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists and scheduled publish times. It is not offered to the public and does not upload content on behalf of third parties.

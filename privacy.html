Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy describes how YT Auto Uploader ("the app") handles data. The app is an internal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the Google accounts / YouTube channels that the owner authorizes.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and title) needed to upload to the correct channel.
  • Information about videos the app itself uploads (video ID, title, status, scheduled publish time).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload videos, set their metadata and thumbnails, add them to playlists and schedule their publication on the authorized channels. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

Tokens and upload logs are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app. Stored data can be deleted at any time by deleting the app's local credentials folder.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties. Data is only sent to Google/YouTube through the official YouTube Data API.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
